# CSE 251B — Deep Learning

**4 units** · CSE · offered varies, varies, varies

## What this course covers

Fundamentals of deep neural networks at the graduate level: multi-layer perceptrons,
backpropagation, automatic differentiation, convolutional networks, recurrent networks,
transformers, and advanced deep learning topics.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- building and training deep neural networks
- modern architectures (CNNs, transformers)

## Topics covered

multi-layer perceptrons, backpropagation, CNNs, RNNs, transformers, automatic differentiation.

## Tools used

Python, PyTorch.

## Prerequisites

CSE 251A (or equivalent ML background).
