# MGT 477 — Consumer Behavior

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered varies, varies, varies

## What this course covers

MBA elective on how consumers perceive, decide, and buy: psychological foundations of
consumer decision-making and their implications for marketing strategy, brand, and
product design.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- translating consumer psychology into marketing and product decisions

## Topics covered

consumer psychology, judgment and choice, branding, persuasion, customer insight.
